# FM-1 Instrument privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026  
Publisher: Oleksii Pitenko

FM-1 Instrument is an independent musical instrument application. It does not require an account and has no advertising or analytics SDK. We do not operate a service that collects your projects, recordings, usage history, identifiers or contact information.

## Your music and settings

Instrument resources, projects, samples and preferences are stored in the application's local container. Import and export use the system file picker and share sheet. A destination you select, such as iCloud Drive or another app, handles the exported file under its own terms. The app does not automatically upload your music to the publisher.

Device and iCloud backups may include app files according to your system settings. Deleting the app removes its local data; system backups are managed separately through Apple settings. There is no app account to delete.

## Downloads and external links

When you choose to install an instrument's resources, the app connects to the release host identified in the instrument listing, such as GitHub or the author's download site. The host receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address, under its own privacy policy. These downloads do not upload your musical projects. Source and support links open an external browser.

The app does not request microphone, contacts or photo-library access. We do not sell personal information or use cross-app tracking.

## Support

You can contact the publisher through [the support issue tracker](https://github.com/HolidayMan/fm1-ios/issues). Issues are public: do not include passwords, private contact information or private music files. Information you voluntarily post there is hosted by GitHub under its privacy policy.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
