Two files of the firmware's HAL are curist's fm1-clk320-probe
<https://github.com/curist/fm1-clk320-probe> (MIT), unchanged:

- firmware/hal/fm1_clk320.h, the switch from the FM-1's 360 MHz to JieLi's rated 320 MHz: the check
  of the clock registers and the switch itself, after the JieLi AC79 SDK's sys_clock_update
  (Apache-2.0)
- firmware/hal/fm1_clk320_probe.h, its reason codes and a loop timed against the crystal

Skiff runs them at boot when MENU > SYSTEM > CPU CLOCK is 320 MHZ (firmware/hal/fm1_clk.h,
firmware/src/main.c). The two files stay under the MIT licence below; the rest of Skiff is
GPL-3.0-only (LICENSE). The files carry no copyright line of their own: their author is curist,
2026, and their header asks that the JieLi credit be kept.
