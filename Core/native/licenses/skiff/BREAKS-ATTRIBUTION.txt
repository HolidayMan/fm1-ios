SLICE's built-in loops (Skiff phase 9, after Skiff 1.1)

  break.wav  BREAK (SLICE SOURCE 0): one bar at 120 BPM, 22050 Hz mono, 44100 samples
  funk.wav   FUNK (SLICE SOURCE 5): two beats at 86.33 BPM, 22050 Hz mono, 30650 samples

Both are cut from drum takes generated on 2026-10-15 with the YuE2 music model by Multimodal Art
Projection (m-a-p, https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE): YuE2-3B (Comfy-Org's
yue2_3b_int8_convrot.safetensors) in ComfyUI 0.37.4's native YuE2 nodes, ABC planning off,
12 s, temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, top_k 100, repetition penalty 1.2; KSampler 32 steps, cfg 1,
dpm_2, sgm_uniform. The style text of both ended in "A drum break played on a live acoustic drum
kit: kick, snare, hi-hats, ghost notes. Drums only, no vocals, no bass, no guitar, no keys.
120 BPM, 4/4, dry vintage soul recording."

  break.wav  style "Breakbeat drum solo. ...", lyrics "[Drum Break] [instrument solo]
             [instrument solo]", seed 1001: its second bar, 2.713 to 4.713 s (it plays at 120.00 BPM)
  funk.wav   style "Solo funk drum break. ...", lyrics "[Instrumental] [Drum Solo]", seed 1000:
             4.473 to 5.863 s (it plays at 86.33 BPM: four eighths, before a sung phrase at 7.3 s)

Each cut: 48 kHz stereo to 22050 Hz (soxr), left and right mixed to mono, its start 8 ms before
the beat, its join cross-faded over 3 ms with what followed the loop's end, peak -0.5 dBFS.
tools/gen_samples.py encodes them (IMA ADPCM) and writes their slice tables.
